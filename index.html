03 · Bonus Technical & Local SEO Cleanup

6 hidden issues we found under the hood (all fixed automatically)

Beyond the 3 visible features above, we ran a technical audit of your site and found several behind-the-scenes bugs left by previous agencies that are holding back your Google rankings.

Fix #1 · Google Indexing

Un-hide your Gallery page from Google

Right now, a broken canonical tag tells Google your Gallery lives at /gallery/ instead of /boston-bluestone-gallery/, dropping it from your sitemap. Plus, its hidden description says "Custom Stone Reception Desk." We fix both.

Fix #2 · South Shore SEO

Connect 20 orphaned town pages + Stoughton

You have 20 town pages (Canton, Sharon, Easton, Norwood, Braintree, etc.) with zero links pointing to them, and no page for Stoughton or Boston. We link all of them cleanly in the footer so they rank locally.

Fix #3 · Brand Heritage

Unify the 4 different founding dates

The homepage says Since 1921, Facebook says 1972, the Contact Google snippet says 1998, and About Us says "over two decades." We tell your 1921 story & 4 brands (The Stone Center, Adamo Stone, Freeport Marble) clearly.

Fix #4 · Google Star Ratings

Fix invalid "Fake SKU" schema code

Your review plugin injects invalid @type: Product code with a fake SKU instead of proper HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema with your Stoughton address and hours (Mon–Fri 7am–3:30pm).

Fix #5 · Polish & Details

Fix blank favicon, typos & hidden links

Your browser tab icon is literally named Blank-Favicon-32x32.png. We also fix copy typos ("to you kitchen", "compliment") and remove secret Facebook links hidden on punctuation marks (! and .).

Fix #6 · Speed & GoDaddy Staging Leak

Remove old staging domain redirect

Every visitor's browser is still loading an old GoDaddy staging file (6vt.b68.myftpupload.com) that slows down the page and triggers browser security warnings. We clean this out completely.
